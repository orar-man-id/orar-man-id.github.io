Add your group to your phone

Each button saves that group's classes (26 sessions) as a calendar file with the room, teacher and both names in every event, plus a reminder 30 minutes before. Pick the button for your phone to get the matching steps.

iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap your group's iPhone button.
  2. Safari shows the classes: tap Add All and pick a calendar.
  3. If it saves to Files instead, open Files and tap the .ics file, then Add All.
Android
  1. Tap your group's Android button and open the downloaded file.
  2. Choose Google Calendar, then Import.
  3. Or open any class here and use Add to Google Calendar for just that session.

Start times, rooms and teachers are copied from the university document “ORAR ID an I SEM i 2026-2027”. The document lists only start times, so end times assume the standard 90-minute session. Check the official document if anything changes.
