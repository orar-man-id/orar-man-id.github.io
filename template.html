Orarul în telefon / Your timetable on your phone

Subscribe to your group and your phone keeps itself up to date: when the faculty moves a class, it moves in your calendar too (iPhone usually within a few hours, Google Calendar within a day). Every event has the room, the teacher and both names, plus a reminder 30 minutes before.

iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap your group's iPhone button.
  2. Confirm Subscribe, then Add.
  3. Or add it by hand: Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, and paste your group's link: …
Android / Google Calendar
  1. Tap your group's Google button and sign in to Google if asked; confirm Add. This works best on a computer.
  2. On the phone, open Google Calendar → Settings, tap the new “Orar Grupa …” calendar and turn on Sync.
  3. If you already imported a file before, delete those events so you don't see each class twice.

The File button downloads a one-time copy instead. It does not update itself.

Start times, rooms and teachers are copied from the university document “ORAR ID an I SEM i 2026-2027”. The document lists only start times, so end times assume the standard 90-minute session. The page checks the document every hour and updates itself. Check the official document if anything looks wrong.
